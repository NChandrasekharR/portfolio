How I Work

Experiment-first mindset

I form hypotheses, test them systematically, and make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.

Make allies & build together

Design doesn't happen only in Figma. I work with partners via workshops, napkin sketches, and corridor chats to get alignment.

Pragmatic design decisions

I spend time deeply understanding systems, tech, and business so we can build something realistic that works for everyone.
