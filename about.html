About

A bit more about me

Hi, I'm Chandra

I'm a product builder, who loves designing with data, experiments & prototypes.

My design career so far

Quizizz (2024 - Present)

I work on AI & enterprise products for schools and districts at Quizizz. I build tools that empower teachers to deliver the best experience for their students in the classroom.

Gojek (2021 - 2024)

I collaborated with business, product & engineering teams to experiment + roll out new ad experiences on the Gojek consumer app that have driven $5m USD in additional revenue till date, while keeping UX at the forefront.

Pause (2020 - 2021)

Pause is a Leave Management System software for small businesses developed by Obvious. I helped design the alpha web app, then helped on UX research and customer ops.

How I work

Experiment-first mindset

As a designer I form hypotheses, test them systematically, and make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.

Make allies and build together

Design doesn't happen only in Figma. Work with partners via workshops, napkin sketches, & even corridor chats to get alignment.

Pragmatic design decisions

I spend lots of time to deeply understand systems, tech and business so that we can build something realistic that works for everyone.

Outside of work

When I'm not designing, you can find me exploring new places, trying out new recipes, or diving into a good book.
